The worlds of jazz and musical theatre have long been intertwined. In collaboration with North Hatley Jazz Festival president Jon Gearey, the Massawippi Theatre Troupe is bringing to the stage both classics of the Great American Songbook as well as show tunes from some big musical hits in this showcase fundraiser.

Tickets will be available at the door if supplies remain. Cash only.
