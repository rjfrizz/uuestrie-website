Jazz in North Hatley — don’t miss it, October 10 at 7pm!

Kate Wyatt & Adrian Vedady featuring Adam Kolker

Following their memorable performance at the second edition of the North Hatley Jazz festival this past August, Kate Wyatt and Adrian Vedady return with a very special guest: acclaimed Brooklyn saxophonist Adam Kolker. Recognized for his work with legendary ensembles such as Ray Barretto, the Maria Schneider Orchestra, and the Village Vanguard Orchestra, Kolker brings a distinctive voice to the trio.

Join the trio for an evening of lyrical depth, rich atmosphere, and pure acoustic intimacy.

Date: Saturday, October 10

Time: 7:00 PM

Location: Unitarian Universalist Church of North Hatley, 201 Main St, North Hatley, QC

Tickets: $20 at the door (Cash or Interac accepted)
