Sunday services

Services are at 10:30 a.m. on Sundays throughout the year, with a break during the last week of December and the first week of January. From April until mid-November our services are held in the historic sanctuary, and over the winter in Stoddard Hall on the lower level. Members and visitors enjoy a time of fellowship with coffee, tea and refreshments following each service.

Children are welcome every Sunday. See Children’s Religious Exploration.

Service du dimanche

Le service du dimanche et le programme pour les enfants sont offerts tous les dimanches à 10 h 30 à l’Église unitarienne universaliste de North Hatley, avec une pause la dernière semaine de décembre et la première semaine de janvier. D’avril à la mi-novembre, les services ont lieu dans le sanctuaire historique; l’hiver, dans la salle Stoddard, au niveau inférieur. Un café suit chaque service.

Les enfants sont les bienvenus tous les dimanches. Voir Exploration religieuse des enfants.

How to get here

North Hatley is a small village at the north end of Lake Massawippi, about 15 minutes’ drive south of Sherbrooke, Québec, and 30 minutes’ drive north of the U.S. border at Derby Line, Vermont. UUEstrie is at 201 Main Street, at the corner of Gagnon Street. Open in Google Maps.

From Sherbrooke/Lennoxville: Go south on Route 143 for 6 km. Turn right onto Route 108 and go 14 km. At the stop sign, turn left onto Main Street. You are in North Hatley. Cross the bridge and turn right. UUEstrie, 201 Main Street, will be on the left, at the corner of Gagnon Street. If the street begins to go uphill, you have gone too far.

From Montreal: Take Autoroute 10 East to Autoroute 55 South. Take the exit for Ste-Catherine-de-Hatley, Route 108. Go east on Route 108 until you reach North Hatley. At the stop sign, continue straight on Main Street. Cross the bridge and turn right. UUEstrie, 201 Main Street, will be on your left, at the corner of Gagnon Street. If the street begins to go uphill, you have gone too far.

From Vermont: Take I-91 North to the border, where it becomes Autoroute 55 North. Continue on Highway 55 for 29 km. Take the exit for Ste-Catherine-de-Hatley, Route 108. Go east on Route 108 until you reach North Hatley. At the stop sign, continue straight on Main Street. Cross the bridge and turn right. UUEstrie, 201 Main Street, will be on your left, at the corner of Gagnon Street. If the street begins to go uphill, you have gone too far.
